## 5. The Final Lesson

> **Good developers are made in the thinking, not just the typing.**

This is the closing message of the entire Super Skill, and it's worth internalising:

- **The typing is downstream of the thinking.** Code is the expression of a decision already made well or badly.
- **The thinking is what separates developers who ship from developers who churn.** Ten well-reasoned projects beat a hundred hastily-typed ones.
- **The thinking is also where safety, quality, and usefulness are decided** — long before any line of code is written.

You've done the unglamorous part. The next part is where it gets fun.
